# NNNN. Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owner (team):** Platform | Identity | …
- **Jira:** PLAT-?, IDN-?

## Context

What situation forced a decision? What constraints apply? Keep it factual.

## Decision

What we decided, stated plainly ("We will …").

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — why not.
- **Option B** — why not.

## Consequences

- Good: what gets easier.
- Bad / accepted cost: what gets harder, what we now have to maintain.
- Follow-ups: tickets this creates.
